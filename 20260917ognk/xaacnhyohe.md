# 报名：7月9日香港大学深圳医院肝癌影像诊疗进展研讨会

> 更新时间：2026-09-17 (UTC+8)

肝癌是亚洲地区高发的恶性肿瘤。而据统计，中国每年新增肝癌人数30万，每年肝癌死亡人数11万，是全球肝癌发病率和死亡率最高的国家。由于目前尚无有效的预防措施，早诊早疗便成为了提高肝癌生存率的主要途径。磁共振成像凭借其高度软组织分辨率优势，促进了肝癌的早期诊疗。为了提供一个及时交流新信息的平台，我们今年隆重推出由港大深圳医院影像科主办的肝癌影像诊疗进展研讨会。此次会议将邀请香港、澳门、广州和韩国的医学影像、肝胆外科及肿瘤放疗等相关领域的专家，旨在探讨肝癌诊疗的新思想，加快此方面信息传播，促进肝癌多学科的合作与发展。

The incidence of liver cancer is very high among  Asia population. According to statistic report, China has the highest incidence and mortality of liver cancer global wide, with annual new cases of 300,000 and annual death of 110,000 of patients with liver cancer.  Because there is lacking of effective prevention, the early diagnosis and treatment of liver cancer become the most important way to reduce its mortality. Due to its excellent soft-tissue resolution, MRI has provided means to achieve earlier diagnosis and more accurate treatment. In order to provide a platform to  quickly exchange new information, an imaging symposium on liver cancer will be organized by HKU-SZH。  Experts from Hong Kong, Macau, Guangzhou and Korea are invited to the meeting, aimed to explore new ideas on imaging diagnosis and treatment of liver cancer,  facilitate spreading of such information，and promote multi-disciplinary collaboration in this field.
